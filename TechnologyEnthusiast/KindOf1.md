## Software Developer ( Activities And Responsibilities )

* Understand the client’s requirements and make strategies to develop and deliver results (applications) as per their requirement
* Design prototypes and write codes for application development
* Perform testing, validation and troubleshooting of any arising issues
* Incorporate new features in existing applications and improve the performance
* Integrate mobile applications with existing networks in the organization
* Understand and manage the application development lifecycle
* Understand security protocols and perform regular updates, enhancing the quality of applications
* Collaborate with team members to offer end-user support and training
* Create technical handbooks, documents and other requirements to educate about coding and designing the application

## Technology Enthusiasts And Technical Writer ( Activities And Responsibilities )

* Creating User Manuals
